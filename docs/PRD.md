# TradeGPT India - Product Requirements Document

## Vision

TradeGPT India is an Agentic RAG-powered Financial Intelligence Copilot that helps Indian retail investors analyze stocks, understand risks, evaluate portfolios, backtest strategies, and learn investing using trusted financial data.

---

## Problem

Retail investors have access to massive amounts of financial information but struggle to convert it into actionable insights.

Information is fragmented across:

* Annual reports
* Earnings calls
* Financial news
* NSE/BSE filings
* Economic reports

Most investors lack the time and expertise to analyze all of it.

---

## Target Users

### Beginner Investors

Need help understanding stocks and financial concepts.

### Intermediate Investors

Need portfolio analysis and risk evaluation.

### Traders

Need strategy backtesting and market insights.

---

## Core Features

### Stock Analyzer

Analyze a company using:

* Financial metrics
* Technical indicators
* News
* Financial documents

Output:

* Bull Case
* Bear Case
* Risks
* Summary

### RAG Research Engine

Ask questions about:

* Annual reports
* Earnings calls
* SEBI regulations
* RBI reports

Receive grounded answers with sources.

### Portfolio Doctor

Analyze:

* Diversification
* Sector Exposure
* Risk Score

### Strategy Lab

Backtest:

* RSI
* MACD
* SMA

Visualize:

* CAGR
* Sharpe Ratio
* Drawdown

### Learning Hub

Explain financial concepts in:

* Beginner mode
* Investor mode
* Analyst mode

---

## Success Criteria

A user can:

1. Analyze a stock
2. Ask questions about company reports
3. Upload a portfolio
4. Backtest a strategy

within 5 minutes.
